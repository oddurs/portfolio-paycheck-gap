## Outcome

PPG graduates from an interesting chart to a maintained public benchmark with explicit revision rules, independent replication, stable release practices, and enough governance for others to cite and contribute to it.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Methodology changes and upstream data revisions follow a published policy.
- [ ] An independent implementation reproduces the released series within declared tolerances.
- [ ] Contribution, citation, ownership, and release procedures are documented.
- [ ] All discrepancies found during replication are resolved or disclosed.
- [ ] A stable 1.0 dataset and methodology are released together.
