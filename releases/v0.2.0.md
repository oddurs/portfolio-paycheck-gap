# PPG Index v0.2.0

This is the first reproducible historical-data release of the
Portfolio-Paycheck Gap Index. It binds package version **0.2.0**, methodology
version **0.1**, source snapshot **`4156e87c2524a9ce`**, tests, and generated
artifacts to one Git tag.

## Coverage and reading

- Quarterly coverage: **1979 Q1 through 2025 Q3** (187 observations).
- Base: **1980 Q1 = 100**.
- Latest PPG: **4217.405914** at 2025 Q3.
- Quarter-over-quarter change: **+7.627364%**.
- Year-over-year change: **+13.979511%**.
- Status: **stale** because the latest mutually complete quarter is 2025 Q3.

BLS did not produce the 2025 Q4 paycheck observation because October 2025 CPS
data were not collected during the federal shutdown. The release has market
data through 2025 Q4 but does not interpolate or carry earnings forward.

## Known limitations

- PPG is not a valuation, market-timing, recession, or return forecast, and it
  is not a complete measure of inequality or affordability.
- The market leg is a broad US-listed-market proxy, not a proprietary
  float-adjusted total-return index. It reconstructs total return by adding a
  disclosed Treasury-bill proxy to JKP excess return; that approximation is not
  the exact risk-free series used by JKP.
- The paycheck leg is the changing cross-sectional median for full-time wage
  and salary workers age 16+, not a worker panel, household income, total
  compensation, or earnings for all adults.
- Source histories may be revised. This release pins exact raw responses and
  fails closed on an unreviewed overlapping revision.
- JKP-derived data and charts are CC BY-NC 4.0, modified, and limited to
  noncommercial use. Federal Reserve attribution and the BLS secondary-use
  notice in `provenance.json` also apply.
